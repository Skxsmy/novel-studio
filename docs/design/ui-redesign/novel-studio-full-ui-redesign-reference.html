Scene 5

By the time Rin reached the west quay, the tide bell had missed three beats. The harbor answered each silence with a blow against the wall, deep enough to shake rust from the warehouse doors.

Marek waited beneath the maintenance arch with one hand on the brass key. He held it too carefully, as if the metal might remember who had taken it.

“The lock listens to pressure,” Rin said. She set her palm against the weather door. The iron was cold, but behind it something moved with the slow weight of a held breath. “The key only tells us when to start.”

Marek looked toward the flooded stair. For the first time that night, his calm failed him. “Then we have less time than I thought.”

Rin heard the missing beat before the bell failed again. She turned the wheel one spoke and felt the whole chamber lean toward balance.
